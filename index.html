FAQ

Common questions

What is FinanceOS?

FinanceOS is a financial operating system that brings all your accounts, spending, budgets, subscriptions, savings goals, and upcoming financial events into one clear, organized view. It helps you understand your money and make better financial decisions.

Is FinanceOS a bank?

No, FinanceOS is not a bank. It's a financial management tool that helps you track and organize your money across all your existing bank accounts, wallets, and other financial sources.

Can I manage multiple accounts?

Yes. FinanceOS supports multiple bank accounts, mobile wallets, cash holdings, and even foreign currency accounts. You can see all your balances in one place and track transfers between them.

Can I track subscriptions?

Absolutely. FinanceOS lets you log all your recurring payments and subscriptions so you can see exactly how much of your income is already committed each month. This makes it easy to spot subscriptions you may want to cancel.

Can I set savings goals?

Yes. You can create multiple savings goals with target amounts and dates. FinanceOS tracks your contributions and shows your progress so you stay motivated and on track.

Can I use FinanceOS offline?

FinanceOS is a progressive web app that can be installed on your home screen. Once installed, core parts of the app load from your device, and your data syncs to the cloud so it's available whenever you need it.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. FinanceOS uses encrypted cloud storage, PIN protection, and secure authentication. We never sell your data or show ads. Your financial information stays private and under your control. You can also export your data at any time.
