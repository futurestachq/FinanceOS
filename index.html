Features

Everything you need to
master your money.

No spreadsheets. No guesswork. Just a clear picture of your money, your goals, and your progress.

Smart Budgets

Set monthly limits for every spending category. Get instant alerts when you are close to overspending.

Savings Goals

Create goals for anything — rent, a car, an emergency fund. Track contributions and watch your progress grow.

Bill Calendar

See all your bills, income, and subscriptions on one calendar. Never miss a due date again.
